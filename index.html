Quick is a secure, QR-based payment method powered by Faster Payment. Customers approve payment using their bank app and biometrics, making safer for them and more cost effective for you. No chargebacks, no frauds

Quick is a flat-fee, QR-first payment method built for in-person service businesses in the UK.

A faster, simpler way to get paid – without cards.

Quick is an alternative payment method using Pay-by-Bank (PBB) rails instead of traditional cards networks. It’s secure, instant and works across all channels: in-store, online, and via payment links.

How it works

The merchant displays a QR code, the customer scans the QR code with their phone – no app download needed

The customer’s banking app opens automatically with payment details already filled in

The customer approves using biometrics (Face ID, fingerprint, or passcode)

The payment is completed instantly and the merchant gets the money right away

Refunds are just as easy

  1. The merchant scans the QR code on the receipt
  2. Approves the refund
  3. The customer gets their money immediately – not in 2-3 days

No cards. No % fees. No waiting.

Just fast, secure payments – powered by bank-to-bank technology
