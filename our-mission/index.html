Our Mission

To offer in-person payment choice to consumers by enabling merchants to get paid instantly and securely — without relying on traditional cards.
We use bank-to-bank technology to reduce costs, speed up settlement, and challenge outdated payment systems.

Our Values

Savings

We replace unpredictable card fees with a low, transparent cost per transaction — helping merchants protect their margins.

Security

Every payment is approved by the consumer using biometrics and their banking app — ensuring trust and fraud protection.

Consumer Choice

We empower customers with a seamless, card-free way to pay in person — building loyalty while driving down cost.
