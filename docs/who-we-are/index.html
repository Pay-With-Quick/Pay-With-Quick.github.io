Who we are

Quick was founded by Delia Pedersoli, a seasoned expert with over 20 years of experience in the digital payments industry. Having worked with global merchants like IKEA, Carrefour, Zara, Mediamarket, and The Frasers Group, Delia understands the complexity and cost of accepting payments, especially in face-to-face and cross-border environments.

She's successfully built and exited multiple fintech startups, and now leads a team of professionals with deep experience in alternative payments, acquiring, and merchant technology.

Frustrated by outdated systems and excessive card fees, she created Quick to challenge the status quo, giving merchants a smarter, fairer way to accept payments.
