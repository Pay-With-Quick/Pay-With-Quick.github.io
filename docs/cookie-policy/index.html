Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on paywithquick.co.uk in line with UK PECR and UK GDPR.

Last updated: 13 March 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and your choices on `paywithquick.co.uk`.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function, remember preferences, and measure performance.

2. Cookies we use

Strictly necessary cookies

Required for core website functionality and security. These cannot be turned off.

Optional analytics cookies

Used only with your consent to understand site usage and improve performance.

3. Consent choices

On first visit, you can accept or reject optional cookies using our consent banner. Your choice is stored and can be changed by clearing site cookies/local storage and reloading the website.

4. Current cookie usage summary

  • Consent preference: stores your cookie decision.
  • Essential operation: supports core page delivery and security.
  • Optional analytics: enabled only if you accept.

5. Managing cookies in your browser

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Contact

For questions about cookies, contact us at info@paywithquick.co.uk.

We use strictly necessary cookies to run this website and optional analytics cookies to improve performance. You can accept or reject optional cookies. See our Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy.
